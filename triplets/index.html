Triplet Annotation

21–22 April 2026, University of Antwerp

For each triplet, decide which document does not belong. Pick the odd one out (A, B, or C); choose “All Same” if all three look like they were written by the same hand; choose “All different” if all three look like they were written by a different hand. Use the controls to zoom and pan each image. Navigate between triplets with the arrow buttons or ← → keys. Write down your opinion in the table on the printed sheet of paper.
